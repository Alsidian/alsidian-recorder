Before you start
Recording Instructions
Read each tip carefully — it takes 1 minute
🔇
✓ Environment
Record in a quiet place
✓Find a quiet room — close the door, and turn off fans, AC, and the TV
✓If using a laptop, sit in a quiet room and speak clearly toward the microphone
✗Never record in a moving car, café, or anywhere with background noise or echo
✗A recording will be rejected if we detect loud background noise, fan/AC hum, TV, traffic, or other voices
📱
✓ Your Device
Set up your device correctly
✓Hold your phone 10–15 cm from your mouth — not too close, not too far. Do not cover the microphone with your fingers
✓Put your phone in Airplane Mode before starting — a notification or call during recording will ruin the file
✓Earphones with a built-in mic (the ones that came with your phone) work perfectly fine
✗A recording will be rejected if a phone notification sound or vibration is heard in it
🗣️
✓ How to Speak
Sound natural, not read-aloud
✓Read the sentence silently first. Understand it fully. Then record — speak it as if saying it naturally in conversation, not reading it aloud
✓Speak at your normal everyday pace — the way you talk to a friend. Not slowly, not dramatically
✓Complete the whole sentence in one go — no pauses mid-sentence. Speak it fluently from start to finish
✗Do not whisper, shout, or put extra stress on any word — speak the whole sentence evenly and naturally
⚠️
✗ What Gets Rejected
Avoid these — recordings will be rejected
✗Stumbling, correcting yourself, or repeating a word — if this happens, press the trash icon 🗑 and re-record
✗Reading too fast or too slowly, or skipping / changing words in the sentence
✗Echo or hollow sound (e.g. recording in a bathroom or empty hall)
✗Recordings under 2 seconds long will be automatically rejected
⏱️
⚙ Start & Stop Timing
These two habits make a big difference
1 After pressing Start Recording — take one natural breath, then begin speaking. This short gap ensures your first word is fully captured.
2 After finishing the last word — pause naturally for one second, then press Stop Recording. This ensures your last word is not cut off.
